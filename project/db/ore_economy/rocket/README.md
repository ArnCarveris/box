# Rocket assembly and launch

Rockets built step by step on a pad, loaded with Luminite, launched, and the life support win.

## Details

Port rocket.c and rocket/*.flecs: the assembly steps and their parts, loading, the launch sequence
with the pad clamps and bell effects, the score from shipped Luminite, and boarding a rocket with a
life support module to end the game.

Verification: Unit tests of the assembly and launch state machines, a command based test launching a rocket, a
visual scene of a launch.
