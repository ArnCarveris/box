# Canal lock

An interactive diorama of a working canal lock: you are the lock keeper, and the job is to
take a narrowboat from the lower canal up to the upper canal and back. Everything is driven by
clicking on the objects in the 3D scene.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
bake3 run apps/boxrun --local-env -- etc/scenes/canal_lock.flecs
```

The panel at the top left always names the next step, and the options panel drives the time of
day. Hold the right mouse button to look around and use WASD, E and Q to fly; `--camera` shows a
camera without the scene's views, so clicking only works in the default view.

## What you can click

| Object | What it does | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift bridge | Raises or lowers the white bridge over the lower canal | Any time |
| Bottom gates (black and white beams) | Swing open or shut | Chamber is low |
| Top gates (black and white beams) | Swing open or shut | Chamber is high |
| Bottom paddle gear (red) | Raises its paddle and drains the chamber | Top paddle down, top gates shut |
| Top paddle gear (red) | Raises its paddle and fills the chamber | Bottom paddle down, bottom gates shut |
| Narrowboat | Sails to the next stop | The way ahead is open |
| Mill sluice | Sends water over the wheel, which turns until you shut it | Any time |
| Cottage door, lantern | Open the door, light the lantern and the cottage windows | Any time |

Objects glow amber under the cursor when they can be used. When a rule forbids it they glow red
and the cursor shows a crossed circle. The signal lamp beside each pair of gates is green when
those gates may be opened. While the water, a gate, the bridge or the boat is moving, clicks on the
lock are ignored.

## How it works

The state is a set of exported `mut` variables in `state.flecs`, which `on.click` handlers write
and templates read reactively. What moves on screen is a `Transition` on `Position` or `Rotation`,
so the state changes at once and the pixels follow. The boat's height uses the same 6 second
transition as the water, so they rise together.

- Hover uses separate `on.enter` and `on.leave` loops. `on.leave` does not fire when an object
  moves out from under a still cursor, so a click that moves an object also clears its hover.
  Reading `PickStatus` instead would re-evaluate every control on every mouse move.
- Script has no timer, so a click handler compares `AppStatus.elapsed` with `lock_busy_until`.
- A control template only holds its reactive root. Its geometry lives in a separate body template:
  anything anonymous directly inside a template that re-evaluates gets a new id, and a press and
  release on different ids is not a click.

## Materials and rendering

The textures in `textures/` are generated by `textures/generate.py` (numpy and Pillow, only needed
to regenerate them); the boat livery and the brass plate are SVG.

| Texture | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `chamber_wall` (albedo, normal, ORM) | One image of the whole chamber wall: dry above the high water line, wet and glossier down to the low water line, slimy below |
| `ashlar`, `brick`, `slate`, `plaster`, `wood`, `bark`, `stone` | Tiling albedo and normal maps for the masonry, roof, gates and timber |
| `ground` | One image of the grass of the whole diorama with the worn paths and wild flowers painted in |
| `strata` | The cut face of the diorama, from the topsoil to bedrock |
| `water_normal` | Ripples, scrolled by a very long `TextureTransform` transition that `SetAt` starts on frame 2 |
| `leaves`, `tuft` | Alpha tested foliage cards. They are near white and tinted by `Rgb`, because a `BackFacePlain` face shows `Rgb` without the albedo texture |

Large surfaces are `Quad`s with a `TextureTransform` sized to keep a constant texture density, because
a `Box` maps its whole UV range onto every face. The earth under them is a solid core inset from the
faces, which keeps shadows and bakes closed without any coplanar faces.

Things to keep in mind when extending the scene:

- A shape needs a `Position` (or a `Rotation`, which implies one) to follow its parent. Without one
  it renders at the world origin.
- Named entities in a `for` loop are one entity, so loop bodies use anonymous entities.
- Faces must not share a plane: separate parallel surfaces by at least 0.01 units.
- The table is `SpatialIgnore`, because the bake grid covers the spatial bounds of the scene. Without
  it the bake tries to fill a 320 unit volume with probes and the app appears to hang.
- Foliage and grass are `SpatialIgnore`: they never block a click, and mouse moves stay cheap.
- The lantern's point light casts no shadows: its shadow buffer has the handle of the first particle
  page (`BOX_RENDER_BUFFER_SHADOW_FIRST` and `BOX_RENDER_BUFFER_PARTICLE_FIRST` are equal), which
  stops the water jets with `particle pool ... does not fit buffer` errors.
- A root level entity carries `SetAt`: a child of its target would copy its `Parent` onto the target.

## Assets

- `state`: the lock state. `control`: hover color and cursor shared by all controls.
- `gate`, `paddle`, `bridge`, `narrowboat`: the lock and the boat, with a lofted hull.
- `cottage`, `mill`: the keeper's cottage, door, lantern and props, and the water wheel and sluice.
- `landscape`, `nature`: terrain, masonry, trees, shrubs, grass, reeds, lilies and ducks.
- `guide`: the panel with the next step.
- `materials`: textures, shared prefabs and `CanalFace`.
